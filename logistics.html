ABOUT PITTIE LOGISTICS

SETTING NEW BENCHMARKS IN THE WORLD OF LOGISTICS

Pittie Logistics is a leading third-party logistics (3PL) provider in India, delivering end-to-end solutions from “storage to stores” across warehousing, transportation, and value-added services. Part of the Pittie Group (est. 1991), we combine decades of supply-chain expertise with advanced technology, a 1000+ strong workforce, and a strong national and global presence to deliver customized, data-driven logistics solutions that drive efficiency and scalable growth.

CARRY & FORWARD PARTNERS

Superstockist

Pittie brings in the financial appetite to cater to the growth of its partner brands. A brand's developing sales momentum calls for strategic financial holdings in inventory to keep the working capital steady and achieve scale. Our team makes this complex model easy by recommending precise stock placement against observed sales & rolling forecasts, operations, fulfilling SLA's in your value chain, and much more.
