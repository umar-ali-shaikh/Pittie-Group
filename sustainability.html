Responsible Business, Sustainable Future

At Pittie Group, we believe business success and social responsibility go hand in hand. Discover how we're making a positive impact.

Our Key Initiative
Our commitment to sustainability drives us to create products that are better for you and better for the planet.
  • 1. Eco packaging
  • 2. Women Empowerment
  • 3. Gau-Based Products
  • 4. Clean India
    • Purposeful Packaging, Designed for Sustainability
    The company focuses on responsible and innovative packaging decisions to reduce environmental impact without sacrificing quality.
    Key measures include:
    • Thoughtful and minimal use of plastic—only where necessary
    • Elimination of all single-use plastics from packaging systems
    • Full compliance with EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) standards
    • Consistent use of recycled materials across packaging components

    The aim is to create a packaging ecosystem that is both efficient and environmentally conscious.
    Empowering Women Through Employment
    Over 60% of our manufacturing workforce are women from rural areas. Our initiatives include:
    • Skill development programs in partnership with local NGOs
    • Flexible work arrangements to accommodate family needs
    • Leadership training for female employees
    • Equal pay certification across all our facilities
    We've helped over 5,000 women gain financial independence through dignified employment.
    GauGaurav: Sacred & Sustainable
    Our GauGaurav line transforms cowdung-based products into premium puja essentials while supporting rural economies:
    • 100% natural cow dung incense sticks and dhoop
    • Chemical-free cleaning products using cow urine
    • Partnerships with 50+ gaushalas across India
    • Fair trade practices ensuring farmers receive 30% above market rates
    This initiative preserves traditional knowledge while creating sustainable livelihoods.
    Clean India Initiative
    Since 2010, our nationwide cleanliness program has:
    • Distributed over 1 million cleaning kits to schools and communities
    • Trained 25,000+ volunteers in waste management
    • Adopted 50 public spaces for regular cleaning
    • Developed biodegradable cleaning solutions for municipal use
    Our goal is to make sanitation accessible and sustainable for all Indians.
